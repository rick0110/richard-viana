Profile

I'm an undergraduate at IMPA Tech (Instituto de Matemática Pura e Aplicada), in the B.Sc. in Mathematics of Technology and Innovation with an emphasis in Data Science — at the intersection of mathematical modeling, algorithms, and applied AI. I currently work as an AI intern at MAG LAB (MAG Group), where I own the architecture of a generative-AI platform in production. Before that came a scientific-initiation project in medical image processing with the IDOR research network, a research internship at IMPA's Center for Projects and Innovation, and a run of competitions, hackathons, and academic-industry partnerships.

My main interests are computer vision and generative and agentic AI — from satellite and medical image classification to multi-agent systems. Alongside that I work across statistical modeling, speech synthesis, and computer graphics, usually implementing the core mechanism myself — attention, rendering, prosody encoders — rather than importing it as a black box.
